## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the "Expressive Warmth" philosophy: friendly, deeply reassuring, celebratory, and radically clear. Tailored for collaborative gifting and group greeting cards without the awkwardness of direct cash transit, the visual language eliminates financial anxiety and elevates anticipation, gratitude, and communal delight.

The aesthetic bridges contemporary digital craft with tangible stationery cues. It blends tactile pill shapes, soft parchment-like surfaces, energetic oversized metrics, and human-centric micro-copy. Every element communicates safety, openness, and celebration—ensuring even first-time group contributors feel entirely confident and emotionally included.

## Colors

The palette establishes a comforting, natural baseline anchored by deep foliage tones and warm celebration highlights.

- **Background Canvas (`#F7F3EB`)**: A cozy, natural warm cream that replaces stark digital white with the tactile texture of fine card stock.
- **Surface & Cards (`#FFFDF8`)**: An illuminated ivory surface slightly elevated above the canvas, bounded by subtle warm framing borders (`#E6DFD5`).
- **Brand Primary (`#033922`)**: A profound forest green conveying institutional stability, trust, and natural elegance. Deepened variants (`#0A3C30` / `#00251C`) provide sharp contrast for text locks and header anchors.
- **CTA Accent (`#FD5D17` / `#FE6A34`)**: A vibrant tangerine orange that pulls the user forward on primary conversion pathways with upbeat urgency and tactile energy.
- **Festive Gold Accent (`#FDB022`)**: Reserved for contribution milestones, greeting card highlights, celebration badges, and gift unlock moments.
- **Typography**: Text Primary sits at `#1A1F1B` (a softened charcoal-forest tint for readability), while Text Secondary employs `#5F6B64` for supportive metadata and micro-labels.

## Typography

The typography pairing generates immediate emotional resonance. **Bricolage Grotesque** brings warmth, lively quirks, and punchy editorial presence to titles and oversized financial trackers. **Plus Jakarta Sans** delivers structural neutrality, openness, and optimal legibility across mobile displays.

Numerical figures—particularly funding quotas, participant headcounts, and deadlines—are prioritized with bold weights and generous scale (`display-counter`) to ensure key data is immediately digestible at a glance.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first fluid layout forms the backbone of the experience. Screens are organized along a disciplined 4-column layout on mobile devices (320px–599px) with 16px gutters and margins, expanding to 8 columns on tablet devices (600px–1023px) and 12 columns on desktop (1024px+) capped at a maximum readability container width of 1120px.

Whitespace is intentional and uncrowded. Component groupings prioritize compact, punchy blocks vertically to avoid endless scrolling during high-intent contribution flows.

## Elevation & Depth

The design system eschews cold, artificial dropshadows in favor of warm ambient diffuse lighting layered over subtle tonal boundaries:

- **Level 0 (Flat Base)**: Pure `#F7F3EB` canvas ground.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks)**: `#FFFDF8` surface encased in a 1px solid `#E6DFD5` border, combined with a diffused warm tint: `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(95, 107, 100, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating & Modals)**: Lifted drawers and sticky action summaries rest on `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(3, 57, 34, 0.10)` surrounded by crisp hairline borders.
- **Level 3 (Tactile Press Response)**: Primary buttons use an active state transform: an inner warm inset shadow paired with a 1px downward translation to mimic physical key presses.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a pill-centric, organic profile that reinforces approachability and tactile comfort:

- **Pills (`rounded-full`)**: Standard for all action triggers, CTAs, status tags, participant pills, and stepper nodes.
- **Extra Soft Containers (`rounded-3xl` / 24px–32px)**: Applied to core product cards, message boxes, and bottom sheets.
- **Media Containers (`rounded-2xl` / 16px)**: Encloses gift preview photography, keeping imagery friendly while preserving its natural framing.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Full pill shape (`rounded-full`), min-height of 48px (52px on mobile flows). Background `#FD5D17`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `Plus Jakarta Sans` 16px bold (`label-lg`). Includes tactile press active state.
- **Secondary Action**: Pill shape, min-height 48px, background `#FFFDF8`, 1.5px solid border `#E6DFD5`, text `#033922`. Hover transition to border `#033922`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: Pill shape, background transparent, text `#033922`, underline on hover.

### Badges & Chips
- Compact pills (height 28px–32px) styled with an integrated icon slot (16px).
- Status variant "Colletta Aperta": `#033922` tint (10% opacity background), `#033922` text.
- Celebration variant "Traguardo Raggiunto": `#FDB022` warm background, `#1A1F1B` text.

### Gift Product Cards
- Built on `#FFFDF8` with 1px `#E6DFD5` border and `rounded-3xl` corners.
- Top section holds an edge-to-edge or cleanly padded 4:3 aspect ratio photograph with `rounded-2xl` masking.
- Content zone pairs high-contrast price badges, compact single-line titles in `Bricolage Grotesque`, and live contribution progress meters.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Radios are 24px circles with a solid `#033922` center pip when selected.
- Checkboxes use `rounded-md` (8px) corners, filled with `#033922` and crisp white check icons. Unchecked borders rest in `#E6DFD5`.

### Input Fields
- Minimum height of 50px with a gentle pill contour (`rounded-2xl`).
- Background `#FFFDF8`, border 1.5px `#E6DFD5`.
- Active focus state: `#033922` 2px ring with zero blur, label floats cleanly above with `label-md`.

### Stepper & Contribution Progress
- Steppers display compact circular checkpoints (32px) linked by a 3px warm track.
- Completed steps display `#033922` with a white checkmark. Current active step pulses softly with `#FD5D17`.
- The live total bar features a dual-tone fill: solid `#033922` transitioning into `#FDB022` at 100% completion.

### Collaborative Greeting Card (Signature Canvas)
- Dedicated card component mimicking fine paper stationery with subtle textured borders, pinned author avatars, and handwritten-style note blocks alongside the group gift summary.
