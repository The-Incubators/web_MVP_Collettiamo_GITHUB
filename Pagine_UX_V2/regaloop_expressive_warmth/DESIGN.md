## Brand & Style

The design system crafts an engaging, delightfully tactile, and celebratory group-gifting ecosystem. Moving far beyond sterile corporate fintech or generic list software, the system treats gifting as an emotional ritual: joyful, transparent, and effortlessly communal. It eliminates the social awkwardness of tracking contributions with lively feedback, charming micro-moments, and dependable, grounded clarity.

### Personality Archetypes
- **Joyful & Celebratory:** Infused with spirited gift motifs, celebratory pill badges, warm cream cards, and vibrant citrus pops that spark instant delight.
- **Trustworthy & Grounded:** Anchored by deep, authoritative forest greens and stable typography that reinforce bank-level peace of mind and financial security.
- **Approachable & High-Character:** Distinctly human and witty, discarding cold neutral gray sheets in favor of buttery surfaces, expressive display fonts, and tactile micro-details.

### Design Movement: Tactile Warmth & Playful Editorial
The design movement blends **Playful Contemporary Editorial** with **Tactile Modern Utility**:
- **Rich Layered Canvases:** Soft organic cream backgrounds (`#F7F3EB`) cradling pure ivory and buttery card surfaces (`#FFFDF8`).
- **Expressive Graphic Elements:** Punchy, character-rich display typography paired with organic rounded geometry and bespoke badge accents.
- **Micro-Tactility:** Soft pill buttons, crisp hairline borders (`#E6DFD5`), and dual-tone ambient shadows that make cards feel physically liftable.

## Colors

The palette establishes an emotional balance between the enduring credibility of botanical tones and the kinetic thrill of gift-giving.

### Key Tokens
- **Primary (`#0A3C30` / Lush Forest Green):** The institutional backbone. Conveys integrity, security, and calm authority. Applied to brand headers, primary call-to-actions, major section headers, and verified states.
- **Secondary (`#FF6B35` / Energetic Tangerine):** The spark of celebration and action. Drives user attention to immediate contributor tasks, dynamic progress fills, and festive interactive highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#F25C05` / Deep Coral Punch):** Provides visual punch for active pressed states, notification pings, milestone confetti badges, and critical action triggers.
- **Neutral Canvas & Surfaces (`#F7F3EB`, `#FFFDF8`):** A warm culinary cream canvas that eliminates cold monitor glare. Cards rendered in `#FFFDF8` stand proudly off `#F7F3EB` with tactile clarity.

### Color Rules
- Never use cold gray tones (`#F3F4F6` or `#E5E7EB`). All neutral borders and utility lines must use `#E6DFD5` or primary forest overlays.
- Emphasize dual-color badge accents: pair `primary_color_hex` glyphs inside `forest-tint` (`#E8F2EE`) circular badges, and `secondary_color_hex` glyphs inside `tangerine-tint` (`#FFF0E8`) pills.

## Typography

The typographic system fuses the personality and expressive curves of **Bricolage Grotesque** for headlines with the clean, friendly, and hyper-legible geometric foundations of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body and interface mechanics.

### Hierarchical Roles
- **Display & Headlines:** Set in **Bricolage Grotesque** with optical tight tracking (`-0.03em`). Its quirky ink-traps and warm flared terminals inject high character and an unmistakable gift-forward narrative into headers.
- **Interface & Running Copy:** Set in **Plus Jakarta Sans**. Its rounded terminals and open apertures offer fluid scanning during quick mobile chat sessions, form fills, and payment confirmations.
- **Labels & Micro-Chips:** `label-sm` utilizes uppercase capitalization with positive letter spacing (`0.05em`) to grant clarity to status tags, step indicators, and progress pills.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a mobile-first, conversational flow that reflows gracefully onto larger desktop presentations.

### Grid & Spatial Architecture
- **Mobile Container (Under 640px):** Single-column layout with 20px (`1.25rem`) gutters, keeping card content thumb-friendly, tightly grouped, and easy to parse in fast chat-to-web transitions.
- **Tablet & Desktop (641px - 1140px):** Content centers within a focused reading envelope (maximum width 720px for gifting flows; 1120px for marketing/dashboard overviews) using 12 fluid columns with 24px-32px gutters.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** 
  - Card internal padding: `space-lg` (24px) for desktop; `space-md` (16px) for compact mobile viewports.
  - Inter-component gaps: `space-md` (16px) for stacked cards and form groups.
  - Section dividers: `space-2xl` (56px) for major contextual transitions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons flat gray silhouettes in favor of organic warmth, pillowy surface stacks, and subtle borders.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base:** `#F7F3EB` (Warm Buttery Sand). Non-elevated, sets the cozy backdrop.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Step Modules, Input Shells):** `#FFFDF8` bounded by a 1px solid `#E6DFD5` border, backed by a warm ambient shadow:
  `0 4px 14px -2px rgba(10, 60, 48, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(10, 60, 48, 0.03)`
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements, Flyouts, Active Cards):** Lifted with:
  `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(10, 60, 48, 0.09), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(10, 60, 48, 0.04)`
  Border remains 1px solid `#E6DFD5`, shifting to 2px solid `#FF6B35` or `#0A3C30` on active selection.
- **Level 3 (Fixed Sticky Trays, Drawers, Floating Bottom CTAs):**
  `0 -6px 24px -4px rgba(10, 60, 48, 0.08)` anchored with a crisp upper border in `#E6DFD5`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a friendly, tactile visual language. Corners are generous without losing their crisp containment.

- **Standard Cards & Modules:** `rounded-lg` (16px / `1rem`) and `rounded-xl` (24px / `1.5rem`), lending cards a soft, friendly look.
- **Buttons, Text Inputs, and Form Targets:** 12px (`0.75rem`) to 16px (`1rem`) for approachable, finger-friendly interaction.
- **Badges, Chips, Step Numbers, and Accents:** Strict pill-shaped (`9999px`) geometries to echo ribbon loops and gift tags.

## Components

### Iconography & Visual Flair
- **Style:** Duotone and solid Lucide/Phosphor rounded icons. Primary glyphs use `#0A3C30` paired with `secondary_color_hex` (`#FF6B35`) accents.
- **Badges & Gift Motifs:** Every major section highlights key points with a circular decorative icon badge (44x44px, `#E8F2EE` background with an inset `#0A3C30` gift/coin/heart icon, or `#FFF0E8` with `#FF6B35`).
- **Ribbon & Loop Elements:** Section titles and cards feature pill tags (`label-sm`, tracking `0.05em`, rounded-full) that mimic playful gift tags.

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Deep Forest Green (`#0A3C30`) background, `#FFFDF8` text, `label-lg` typography. 52px height with 24px horizontal padding and rounded-xl (14px) corners. Hover shifts to `#072B22` with a subtle +2px vertical lift and shadow.
- **Celebration / Action CTA:** Tangerine (`#FF6B35`) fill with `#FFFFFF` text and subtle glow. Used for primary participant contribution and campaign launches. Hover shifts to `#F25C05`.
- **Secondary Ghost:** Warm cream card fill (`#FFFDF8`), 1.5px `#E6DFD5` border, `#0A3C30` text. Hover adds a 1px drop tint of `#E8F2EE`.

### Step-by-Step Cards
- **Structure:** Multi-step progression stacks framed in `#FFFDF8` cards.
- **Badge Anchor:** Left column features a 36px circular pill badge containing the bold step number (`#0A3C30` on `#E8F2EE`), connected visually to subsequent steps via a soft vertical dashed `#E6DFD5` track.
- **Card Content:** Bold `headline-md` step heading in Bricolage Grotesque, followed by a reassuring body-md description. Includes a decorative micro-pill tag (e.g., "Zero fees", "Instant share") aligned top-right.

### Form Inputs
- 52px height, `#FFFDF8` surface with a 1.5px `#E6DFD5` border and 12px corners.
- Font: `body-md` in `#1A2421`. Placeholder text in `#56635E`.
- Focus state activates a 2px `#0A3C30` border with a soft `#E8F2EE` outer ring (3px spread).

### Cards & Money Pots
- Cards use `#FFFDF8` with a 1px `#E6DFD5` border and Level 1 elevation.
- Progress bars feature a warm sand trough (`#E6DFD5`) with a rounded gradient fill transitioning from `#FF6B35` to `#F25C05`.
- Active participant chips display avatar circles wrapped in a 2px `#FFFDF8` ring with a green presence dot.
